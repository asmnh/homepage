This is the first article on **Asmo.dev**.

The site is generated from Markdown, committed to Git and rendered as ordinary
static HTML.

## Current architecture

```text
Markdown
   ↓
Astro
   ↓
Static HTML
   ↓
Someone's questionable crawler
```

Nothing important requires JavaScript.

## Why this exists

Writing things down is useful.

Publishing them somewhere crawlable is occasionally even more useful - Google is good backup tool, and it's free.

Look how fast they grow - they even publish automatically!
